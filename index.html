TICKEY LAB / STEP 1

Bluetooth connection test

Open this page in Bluefy on your iPhone. This tests your existing BLE firmware. Photo transfer is not included yet.

  1. Disconnect TICKEY-BLE in nRF Connect.
  2. Power the ESP32. If discovery has expired, send b in Serial Monitor, or press the board’s RESET button. Do not reset during a display refresh.
  3. Connect below and choose TICKEY-BLE.

Not connected.

The display button sends B:Hello Fariz! once and replaces the current image with black text. Wait for the panel to finish, then read status again. A successful Bluetooth write alone does not prove the panel refreshed.
