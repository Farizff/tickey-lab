Bluetooth text + photos

Uses your existing epaper_ble_images firmware—no reflash needed. Text is rendered on your phone and sent as an image. Open in Bluefy and keep this tab in front while sending.

Disconnect nRF Connect first. If discovery expires, send b in Serial Monitor or reset the board while the panel is idle.

No selection: formatting applies to the whole message and new text.

Select text before changing font, size, colour or emphasis to format only that selection. With no selection, changes apply to the whole message. Pasting inserts plain text. Background and alignment apply to the whole message. Preview shows the exact transmitted pixels in upright reading order; text compensates for the panel’s upside-down mounting. Portrait reflows to 128 × 296. Photo orientation is independent. Auto-fit is off by default; enabling it shrinks only overflowing text, preserves original mixed sizes, and restores them when space permits or auto-fit is disabled. Abel, Lobster and Pacifico are bundled under the SIL Open Font License; system fonts and unsupported glyphs vary by phone. Bold/italic variants of bundled fonts are browser-synthesized. Overflow or invisible text blocks sending.

Orientation uses the same corrected upright view as text. Portrait crops to 128 × 296 without stretching. Preview shows the exact transmitted pixels in reading order.

Generated locally; no link is opened or sent to a web service. Black on white, error correction M, four-module quiet border and at least 2 pixels per module. Long content is rejected rather than made unreadable. Scan the preview and the physical panel before relying on it.

Choose a photo. Nothing is sent until you tap Send photo.

A transfer changes the current picture only after validation. Disconnecting before commit discards the partial image. Once panel refresh starts, disconnecting cannot undo it. A progress bar reaching 100% does not mean the panel is finished.
