Bluetooth text + photos

Uses your existing epaper_ble_images firmware—no reflash needed. Text is rendered on your phone and sent as an image. Open in Bluefy and keep this tab in front while sending.

Disconnect nRF Connect first. If discovery expires, send b in Serial Monitor or reset the board while the panel is idle.

Not connected.

Formatting applies to the whole message. Preview below is the actual 296 × 128 screen image and updates as you type. Font availability varies by phone; emoji may not reproduce well. Overflow prevents sending.

Choose a photo. Nothing is sent until you tap Send photo.

A transfer changes the current picture only after validation. Disconnecting before commit discards the partial image. Once panel refresh starts, disconnecting cannot undo it. A progress bar reaching 100% does not mean the panel is finished.
